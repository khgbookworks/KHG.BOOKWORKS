About Harun

The person behind KHG Bookworks.

Hello, I am Harun.

I run KHG Bookworks, where I help authors turn finished drafts into books that are ready to publish. I work on editing, cover design, formatting, beta reading and launch material.

I treat every manuscript as the author's own. My job is to make the book clearer, better looking and easier to read, while keeping your voice.
